# b77 — base b76 preserved

ROM: `wwf_b77.bin` (1,572,864 bytes). MD5: `947bdf6ccc66d33c7d9ecc526e3e8e97`.
Base intact: `/workspace/b76/wwf_b76.bin`, MD5 `49075e62b6b23d071da6035564adcea8`.

Only three changes: referee during the count, sitting/layers of Warrior's pin, pin exit for both. No changes to AI, HOME, corner posts, logo, crowd, sound or other moves. The old sprite resources are identical prefixes; only pose 778 is added.

## 1. Referee: piece corruption, not a supposed per-line limit

On Mega Drive the referee occupies two **non-contiguous** blocks: tiles 1088–1115 and 1512–1524. The old drawing translated only the first tile of each piece and then added offsets as if the block were continuous. Some pieces of poses 503, 506 and 507 cross logical tile 28; then foreign tiles were read (even rope tiles), regardless of where the wrestlers were.

`rfColumn` cuts the columns exactly at that VRAM jump. It also translates the offsets before —not after— the rope priority splitting. The palettes, the priority bit, the figures in front and the b76 referee positions are kept.

The exhaustive check of all the tiles of poses 500–509 finds **36 wrong addresses in the old calculation and 0 in the new one**. The symptoms have not been covered up by hiding the referee or moving him elsewhere.

Source for the arcade drawing order: `analisis/arc_rec.dis`, 0x43D4–0x4508 (entry with X, Y and Z separate), 0x45AE–0x45FA (order by floor Y; comparison at entry+8), 0x45FC–0x4614 (Z is added only for projection). Figures: 0xE02C and 0x2549E; the transparent background of `pin_glyph.bin` does not change.

## 2. Warrior covering: Z and two arcade layers

The real table **0xBEF2**, row Warrior 0 / column Duggan 3, gives **dx=32 and dz=16**, not dz=13. At 0xBDCC–0xBE84:

- Warrior (id 0): Y = covered wrestler's Y +1.
- Initial Z = 0xBB−16 and then 16 is added twice: **Z=203**.
- Main pose 0x7F: 0xBD96.
- Auxiliary object 0x1C0AB2, pose 0x80: 0xBE84–0xBEC0, same X/Z and body Y−2.

The historical WPIN track already had the Z folded into its pixels. b75/b76 additionally applied the fallback 174 to it: they lowered it another 13 pixels. That fallback has not been changed for Duggan or for the holds.

For Warrior's sitting, pose **778** is now used, converted directly from arcade poses 0x7F and 0x80. Its 48 tiles are loaded together, but drawn in two passes: body behind the covered wrestler and hands in front, by their respective floor Y. During the historical WPIN approach the folded Z is not duplicated. The existing X centring of the pin is kept.

The converter is `tools/gen77_pin.py`; it checks that the program used by the decoder matches `analisis/tools/prog.bin` exactly. The union of the 0x7F/0x80 masks, with the original offset and Z, matches the historical mask of pose 515. There are no pixels drawn or generated by AI.

Captures: `shots/b77_01_warrior_pin.png`, `b77_02_warrior_cuenta2.png`, `b77_06_antes_despues_pin.png` (same fixture in b76 and b77). Emilio's capture in MAME is the visual reference; the port's camera, scale and palette have not been changed.

## 3. Kicking out and being thrown off: ticks, placement and velocity

Primary source: `analisis/arc_rec.dis` and `analisis/tools/prog.bin`:

- **0xB012–0xB04C**: the covered wrestler moves ±8 once.
- **0xB0EA**: pose 0x9F for 8 ticks and 0xA0 for 4 ticks. That is **16+8 frames**, not the 8+4 frames of b76.
- During the first pose, the covering wrestler still keeps the pin. **0xB0A0–0xB0E4** only places him in the second pose: 0x9B, covered wrestler's X±0x28, Y−1, Z+4. b76 changed it from the start.
- **0xB068–0xB084**: covered → action 5; covering → action 8 sub 0x1C, turned.
- **0x9B66 / 0x9BBE / 0x9C14**: initial displacement ±0x10 and Z+0x18.
- Table **0xA74A[0x1C] → 0xA8E6**, physics parameter 0x21; **0x2814[0x21] → 0x29B4**: vx=0x100, vy=0, vz=0x100, g=0x40. These are values set by the arcade, not the gravity/vz assumed in the b76 notes.
- **0x27D2–0x280E** loads those values; **0x3E20–0x3E8C** applies X/Z and gravity once per tick (two frames). b76 did not move X in flight and started with vz=0.
- **0x9E4C**: 0x9B/0x9C according to height when falling. **0x9C5A–0x9C92**: displacement ±0x20 on touching the floor, sub 0xC. **0xA4C8–0xA53E**: small bounce with vz=0x100/g=0x30.
- **0xA90E–0xA954**, table 0xAA00, index 0: getting up 8 ticks = 16 frames, only for these exits. No dazes are added.

The exit uses the visible origin of the covered wrestler, without suddenly moving it to the pin's corrected origin (the `lyo` correction was only for drawing). The signs are converted to the port's X axis separately from the drawing flip. The pin update is done once before both actors: it does not depend on whether Duggan or Warrior covers, nor on the loop order.

Captures/sheets: `shots/b77_04_warrior_lanzado.png`, `b77_05_duggan_lanzado.png`, `b77_07_kickout_warrior.png`, `b77_07_kickout_duggan.png`.

## Build and checks

- Local SGDK, `PATH=/tmp/marsdev/mars/m68k-elf/bin:$PATH`.
- `out/res/res.o` must be rebuilt: the copy from b76 brought a `.d` with absolute b76 paths. It is already rebuilt with dependencies exclusive to b77. No ROM with old resources is delivered.
- Final build without diagnostic instrumentation: `BUILD_b77.log`, no errors. Inherited warnings about indentation/unused variables.
- `python3 tools/verify77.py`: verifies the base, the sprite prefixes, resources outside the scope and the referee's addressing. Result: `VALIDACION_b77.txt`.
- MAME fixtures for Genesis: `test/fixture77.lua`, `test/fixture77.sh`. Examples: `test/fixture77.sh Wvalidated 1 1 1` (three count), `test/fixture77.sh Dvalidated 0 0 1`, `test/fixture77.sh WKvalidated 1 1 0` and `test/fixture77.sh DKvalidated 0 0 0` (exits). They reach their ends without timeout. Captures are from the emulator, not recreations.
- The fixtures place the pair and prepare its state to repeat the case; the delivered ROM does not contain those shortcuts. The CSVs read RAM during the frame and may show a SAT list still being built: they are not used as proof of absence of sprites. The attempt to read the VDP's `videoram` space returned zeros and **does not constitute a hardware validation**. The exhaustive address check is independent of that attempt, and the captures have been reviewed.
- The auxiliary diagnostic arcade run warned of a checksum on `24j6-0.112`; it is not used to import art or fix constants. The constants above come from the binary/disassembly in `analisis`, and the art from its same decoder.

## Limits of this delivery

It is not a validation of every case in the game nor a promise of global equivalence with the arcade. The repeatable count and exit cases described were verified. The earlier decisions on when to kick out (automatic first count), the duration of lying and the port's rope limits/falls are kept; its collision machine has not been extended. GameFAQs was not needed for these three fixes.
